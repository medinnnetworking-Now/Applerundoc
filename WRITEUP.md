## About the game

RundocRun turns a hospital corridor into a chase. You are the apple. Ten specialists, one for each subject below, run from you around a ward maze. Four are out at once, and the pack gets quicker as it thins.

The ten doctors: Anatomy, Biochemistry, Physiology, Pathology, Microbiology, Pharmacology, Community Medicine, Forensic Medicine, ENT and Ophthalmology.

**The trick to every doc:** catching a doctor is only half the job. Each one quizzes you straight away, with 12 seconds to answer.

| | Points |
|---|---|
| Catch a doctor | +50 |
| Right answer | +200 |
| Speed bonus on a right answer | up to +100 |
| Wrong answer or timeout | 0, and +5 seconds on the clock |
| Time bonus at the end | 2000 minus 15 per chase second |

The chase clock only runs while you chase, so thinking about a question costs nothing. Your final score sets your rank: Fresher, Intern, Resident, Consultant, Head of Department.

**Three levels.** Ward is the classic corridor. ICU adds loops and quicker doctors. Theatre is an open floor with sprinting doctors and 8 seconds a question. Pick any level from the start. Each level has its own rounds board, so Ward, ICU and Theatre each crown their own fastest chaser.

**Share and challenge.** After a run, or at any point mid-chase, players can share a "Beat my time" card with a QR code and a challenge link. Friends who open it see the time they have to beat.

**One shared board.** Scores from every phone land on the same rounds board, so a stall, a hostel or a class can compete live.

## Under the hood

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript on a canvas. No framework, no build step. The shared rounds board runs on Supabase, and the site is hosted on Vercel.
